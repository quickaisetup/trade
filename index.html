01 · Morning Gate — Your pre-flight check
Before you touch a single trade, tick both boxes here. First: check if FTMO has any maintenance or trading restrictions today (takes 10 seconds). Second: scan the economic calendar for high-impact news — a surprise Fed speech or NFP can wipe your stop in seconds. If there's a red news event within 30 min of your planned entry, wait it out. Both boxes green = you're cleared to trade. Leave them red and you're flying blind.

02 · Top-Down Analysis — Build your bias from the top
Start with the big picture and work down: Monthly → Weekly → Daily → 4H → 1H. For each timeframe, click Bull / Bear / Range and set your indicators (EMA, RSI, MACD). The score bar at the bottom adds it all up — green score means conditions favour a long, red means short, near zero means stay out. Don't skip timeframes. A perfect 1H setup against a Daily downtrend is a trap. Once your score is set, the AI Edge button unlocks and gives you personalised coaching based on your own past trades.

03 · Key Levels — Mark your battlefield
Add the price levels you actually care about today — major support/resistance, yesterday's high/low, weekly open, liquidity pools. Use green for long zones (areas you'd want to buy from) and red for short zones (areas you'd want to sell from). This isn't busywork — if a trade takes you away from these levels, you probably shouldn't be in it. Add trendlines and extra context in the notes box below. Takes 2 minutes and saves you from chasing price all day.

05 · Kill Switch — Your circuit breaker
This section watches your numbers in real time and tells you when to stop trading. Three things tracked: how many of your own trades you've taken today (max 1 + 1 bonus), how many losses in a row, and your total P&L in R. If any number hits its limit, close the session — no exceptions. The kill switch button at the bottom lets you manually end the session too. Professional traders protect their capital first. Blowing the daily limit chasing a loss is the #1 FTMO account killer.

07 · End of Day — Close the loop
This is the most important habit you can build. Answer Q1 honestly about what went right — not just wins, but good process (waited for confirmation, sized correctly, followed your rules). Q2 is one thing to fix — just one, be brutally specific. Set tomorrow's goal, pick whether you followed your rules, then hit Save Day. This data feeds your AI briefing tomorrow morning. Traders who journal consistently improve measurably faster — your streak and rank track this exact habit.
